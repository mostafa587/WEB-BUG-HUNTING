
Vulnerability Report
Title: Username Enumeration via Error Messages on Login & Password Reset
Target URL: https://ai-centre.pk/wp-login.php
Vulnerability Type: Information Disclosure / Username Enumeration
Severity: Low (may escalate in combination with other attacks)

✅ Summary:
During testing, it was identified that the login and password reset functionalities on the target website disclose whether a given username or email exists in the system. This behavior allows an attacker to enumerate valid usernames and emails registered on the platform by observing the error messages returned.

✅ Vulnerable Endpoint:
Login Page: /wp-login.php

Password Reset: /wp-login.php?action=lostpassword

✅ Proof of Concept (PoC):
1. Login Attempt:
Request:

plaintext
Copy
Edit
POST /wp-login.php HTTP/1.1
Host: ai-centre.pk
...
username: sdf
password: randompassword
Response:

"Error: The username sdf is not registered on this site. If you are unsure of your username, try your email address instead."

This confirms that the username does not exist.
